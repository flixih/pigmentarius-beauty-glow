Hair, brows and skin in western Puerto Rico

Founded by Wildaliz Arroyo, Pigmentarius has served the west coast of Puerto Rico for more than 20 years. We are specialists in brows, microblading and permanent makeup, and we also offer haircuts, color, highlights and balayage, keratin, hair botox, repair treatments and Brazilian blowout.

Laser hair removal and more

We offer laser hair removal by area (face, upper lip, chin, underarms, full legs, bikini, Brazilian and full body). It requires several sessions and results vary by person. We also do shellac/gel, pedicures and rejuvenating facials. We do not publish prices: call for pricing and a consultation.

Visiting from Mayagüez, Rincón or Aguadilla

We are inside Plaza del Valle Mall, Suite 1, with mall parking. From Mayagüez it is about 15–20 minutes via PR-2, from Rincón about 25–30 minutes and from Aguadilla about 35–40 minutes. Great for tourists staying on the west coast who want a hair or brow appointment during their trip.
